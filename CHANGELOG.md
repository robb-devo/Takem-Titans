# Changelog

## 2.1.0

Start a new world for this version: towns are generated with the new layout.

### Titans
- New titan body on the same skeleton: waist and ribcage, chest, shoulders, neck and nape, fingers that open and clench round what they grab, toes, kneecaps and teeth.
- Each kind wears its own parts: the Armored Titan's hardened plates, the Beast's shaggy coat, the Jaw Titan's bone mask and claws, the Cart Titan's pack and helmet, spikes down the Ravager's back, long and messy hair.
- Every skin repainted at four times the resolution, with muscle definition, faces, rock, fur, bone and lava materials. Boss eyes and the Ravager's cracks glow in the dark.
- They move more like something alive: hips swing, weight shifts while standing, forearms dangle, chests breathe, and the mindless ones jerk their heads.
- A dead titan's skin boils away to raw muscle while steam pours off every limb.
- **Marked titans**: once the story is past the walls, some titans turn up Hardened, Frenzied, Regenerating, Towering or Scalding. They fight differently and pay out experience, Merit Badges and rare kit. A new contract sends you hunting them.

### Towns
- Each district's main square is built: a fountain with benches and a ring of trees (Mitras gets its royal fountain back).
- Plane trees, benches and flags along the main avenue, a market of striped stalls inside each gate, Garrison tents and stores along the walls.
- Houses get dormer windows, shop awnings with wares outside, balconies and a style of their own.
- Timber-framed houses rebuilt: jettied upper storeys on carved brackets over a stone ground floor, St. Andrew's crosses and chevrons in the panels, timber gables, rafter ends under the eaves and a hand-laid stone plinth.
- New building blocks: white, cream and ochre lime plaster, plaster with timber braces, and clay, dark and slate roof tiles with stairs and slabs.

### Sound
- Every core sound rebuilt in layers with several takes each: titan steps, roars, groans, screams and deaths, blades, the nape kill, ODM hooks, winch, gas and wind, explosions, collapses, glass, boulders, cannons and the alarm bell. They play at the levels of the 2.0 mix.
- A score that follows the fight: a calm theme now and then, a danger drone when titans are about, war drums in battle and a new boss theme.

### Gear, look and feel
- New 3D blades for every tier.
- Everything you wear repainted: the Survey Corps jacket with the Wings of Freedom on back, chest and shoulder, the green Vanguard coat and steel helmet, the crystal Hardened set, the recon hood, buckled knee boots, white trousers with thigh straps, and every ODM rig tier in its own finish.
- The cloak falls over the shoulders and carries a large emblem.
- All 71 item icons redrawn in one style, each recognisable at a glance.
- All 21 townsfolk, soldiers, builders and story characters repainted with faces, hair and the uniforms and emblems of their regiments.
- Your scout horse wears the Survey Corps barding: a green saddle cloth with a gold border and the Wings of Freedom on both flanks, a breast collar, a girth and white leg wraps.
- A new title screen: the camera turns over Shiganshina at sunset while the Colossal Titan looks over the wall.
- A nape kill erupts: a flash, more blood the bigger the titan, and blood and steam pouring out of the cut as it falls.
- Wounds on titans are clean gashes with blood running down, no more broad red blotches.
- Small UI fixes: the skill tree's emblem no longer covers its title, locked shop items are dimmed with a readable label, and the field manual's subtitle fits its page.

### Meteor Dive
- The Walls stop it dead: no crater, shock wave, flying rubble or dust gets past a Wall, and the Walls now reach down to the bedrock, so nothing digs under them.
- A tighter blast and crater, still a hammer blow: one deep impact sound instead of a stack of thunder, a low fireball and rolling dust instead of a pillar into the sky and a mushroom cloud, no lightning, and a fraction of the debris and particles for smooth frames.

### ODM gear
- A rush of air at the bottom of every fast swing.

### Cannons
- New gun sounds: the sharp crack of the muzzle blast, the thump in the chest, a roar rolling out over the land with echoes coming back off the hills. The heavy gun and the battery have their own, and the reload is new.
- Gun crews take their time between shots and fire far less often.
- The cannons are repainted: a bronze field gun, a banded heavy iron gun and a blackened three-barrelled battery, on oak carriages with iron straps and spoked wheels with iron tyres.
- Garrison cannons stand on stone gun ledges built out from the face of the wall (on the inside of the arena wall) and only fire on their side. Shells no longer blow up on the wall right at the gun.

### Destruction
- Collapses no longer chain through whole districts: one ruin can bring down what was joined to it, but no further.
- Furniture only stands on the floor under it, and the boards next to a wall piece that falls give way too: a storey follows its walls down instead of hanging in the air until the last wall is gone.
- Corner posts and thin stretches of wall left standing alone after a collapse topple over as one piece.
