# Changelog

## 2.1.0

Start a new world for this version: towns are generated with the new layout.

### Titans
- New titan body on the same skeleton: waist and ribcage, chest, shoulders, neck and nape, fingers that open and clench round what they grab, toes, kneecaps and teeth.
- Each kind wears its own parts: the Armored Titan's hardened plates, the Beast's shaggy coat, the Jaw Titan's bone mask and claws, the Cart Titan's pack and helmet, spikes down the Ravager's back, long and messy hair.
- Every skin repainted at four times the resolution, with muscle definition, faces, rock, fur, bone and lava materials. Boss eyes and the Ravager's cracks glow in the dark.
- They move more like something alive: hips swing, weight shifts while standing, forearms dangle, chests breathe, and the mindless ones jerk their heads.
- A dead titan's skin boils away to raw muscle while steam pours off every limb.
- **Marked titans**: once the story is past the walls, some titans turn up Hardened, Frenzied, Regenerating, Towering or Scalding. They fight differently and pay out experience, Merit Badges and rare kit. A new contract sends you hunting them.

### Towns
- Each district's main square is built: a fountain with benches and a ring of trees (Mitras gets its royal fountain back).
- Plane trees, benches and flags along the main avenue, a market of striped stalls inside each gate, Garrison tents and stores along the walls.
- Houses get dormer windows, shop awnings with wares outside, balconies and a style of their own.

### Sound
- Every core sound rebuilt in layers with several takes each: titan steps, roars, groans, screams and deaths, blades, the nape kill, ODM hooks, winch, gas and wind, explosions, collapses, glass, boulders, cannons and the alarm bell.
- A score that follows the fight: a calm theme now and then, a danger drone when titans are about, war drums in battle and a new boss theme.

### Gear and effects
- New 3D blades for every tier and HD icons for blades, ODM gear, thunder spears and gas canisters.
- Blasts leave a column of smoke.

### Fixes
- Collapses no longer chain through whole districts: one ruin can bring down what was joined to it, but no further.
