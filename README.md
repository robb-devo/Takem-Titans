# Takém Titans

**Attack on Titan in Minecraft.** Swing between rooftops on ODM gear, hunt titans that only die to a cut at the nape, and fight through a full story campaign across the Walls while whole cities come down around you.

## Features

- **ODM gear.** Grapple, reel, boost with gas and swing through towns and forests. There are three gear tiers, up to the supersonic Godspeed rig.
- **Real titan fights.** Titans only die to a nape cut. You can slice their limbs, tendons and eyes to slow them down. They grow tougher as you progress.
- **Bosses.** Fight the Armored, Beast, Ravager, Colossal and Wall titans, each with its own attacks and cutscenes.
- **Destructible world.** Buildings carry real weight. Break a pillar and the house above comes down in chunks, with dust, debris and rumble. Destroyed towns slowly rebuild themselves.
- **Story campaign.** More than 30 stages from training to the final wall, with voiced-style dialogue, cutscenes, districts to defend and repeatable contracts at the end.
- **Gear you earn.** Earn badges from kills and missions, then buy your way up at the quartermaster. You can buy 6 blade tiers, from Standard to the legendary Founder's Edge with its lightning waves. There are also 4 armour sets, cannons, thunder spears and explosives.
- **Skills and ranks.** Level up, unlock skills and climb from Trainee to legend.
- **Your scout horse.** Unlock a loyal horse that builds a gallop charge and senses titans nearby.
- **Custom title screen and New Adventure.** Start straight into Shiganshina with one click.
- English and German included.

## Requirements

- Minecraft **1.21.1**
- **NeoForge 21.1.256** or newer
- At least **4 GB RAM** recommended

## Install

- **Modrinth or CurseForge app:** import the modpack file and press Play.
- **Vanilla launcher:** unzip the installer and double-click `Install-Takem-Titans.bat`.
- **Manual:** drop the jar into the `mods` folder of a NeoForge 1.21.1 profile.

## Building

`gradle build` with Java 21. The jar lands in `build/libs/`.
